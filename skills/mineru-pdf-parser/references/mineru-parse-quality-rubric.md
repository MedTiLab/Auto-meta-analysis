# MinerU Parse Quality Rubric

## good

- Title and abstract are detected.
- Section structure is intact.
- Tables are extracted clearly.
- Figures are separated cleanly.
- Page mapping is present and usable.
- Garbling is low.

## usable

- Most core sections are recovered.
- Some formatting loss is present.
- Table extraction is partial but understandable.
- Page mapping is usable.

## poor

- Large sections are missing.
- OCR or layout recovery is heavily garbled.
- Tables or figures are badly mangled.
- Manual review is needed before evidence extraction.

## failed

- No useful text is recovered.
- The parse output is incomplete or unreadable.
- The task failed or produced no usable artifact.
