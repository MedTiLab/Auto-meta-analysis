## Comparison with previous studies

Compare cautiously. If no previous studies are in the project library, insert a citation placeholder.

## Possible mechanisms

## Limitations

Discuss heterogeneity, cutoff variation, assay differences, sample type differences, population spectrum, and study quality.

## Future research

Recommend large prospective studies, standardized assays, prespecified cutoffs, and external validation.
